Article ID: KB-NET-001
Title: Connection timed out
Product: Customer Portal
Module: Network
Error Codes: TIMEOUT, HTTP 504

Symptoms:
- The operation takes too long
- A connection timed out message appears

Root Causes:
- Backend service is unavailable
- Network connection is unstable
- Request exceeded the timeout

Recommended Solutions:
1. Check the network connection
2. Retry the operation
3. Check backend service health
4. Collect logs if the issue persists

Resolution Verification:
- Retry and confirm a successful response
