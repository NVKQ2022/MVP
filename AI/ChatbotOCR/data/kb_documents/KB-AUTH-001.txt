Article ID: KB-AUTH-001
Title: Invalid or expired authentication token
Product: Customer Portal
Module: Authentication
Error Codes: AUTH-401, HTTP 401

Symptoms:
- Invalid token message
- User is redirected to the sign-in page

Root Causes:
- The access token expired
- The Authorization header is missing
- The device clock is incorrect

Recommended Solutions:
1. Ask the customer to sign out and sign in again
2. Verify the Bearer token is present
3. Check the device date and time

Resolution Verification:
- Retry the operation
- Confirm the request completes successfully
