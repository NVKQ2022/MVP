Article ID: KB-AUTH-002
Title: Customer account is locked
Product: Customer Portal
Module: Authentication
Error Codes: AUTH-403, ACCOUNT-LOCKED, HTTP 403

Symptoms:
- Customer cannot sign in
- Account locked message is displayed

Root Causes:
- Too many unsuccessful sign-in attempts
- An administrator locked the account

Recommended Solutions:
1. Verify the customer's identity
2. Ask an administrator to unlock the account
3. Reset the password if required

Resolution Verification:
- Confirm account status is active
- Ask the customer to sign in again
