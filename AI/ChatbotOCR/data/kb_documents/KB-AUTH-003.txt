Article ID: KB-AUTH-003
Title: Email address has not been verified
Product: Customer Portal
Module: Authentication
Error Codes: EMAIL-VERIFY-001

Symptoms:
- Sign-in is blocked
- Email verification required message appears

Root Causes:
- The verification link was not opened
- The link expired

Recommended Solutions:
1. Resend the verification email
2. Ask the customer to use the newest verification link

Resolution Verification:
- Confirm the user profile shows a verified email
