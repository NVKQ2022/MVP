Article ID: KB-GEN-001
Title: Resource not found
Product: Customer Portal
Module: General
Error Codes: HTTP 404, RESOURCE-404

Symptoms:
- Requested page or record cannot be found

Root Causes:
- URL is incorrect
- Resource was removed
- Customer lacks access to the expected tenant

Recommended Solutions:
1. Verify the URL or record ID
2. Confirm the resource still exists
3. Confirm the active tenant

Resolution Verification:
- Open the verified resource URL
