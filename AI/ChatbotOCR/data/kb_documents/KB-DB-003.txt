Article ID: KB-DB-003
Title: Database deadlock detected
Product: Customer Portal
Module: Database
Error Codes: DB-DEADLOCK-001

Symptoms:
- A transaction is rolled back
- Deadlock message appears

Root Causes:
- Concurrent transactions lock resources in conflicting order

Recommended Solutions:
1. Retry the transaction once
2. Collect the deadlock report
3. Escalate recurring deadlocks to engineering

Resolution Verification:
- Confirm the retried transaction completes
